Method and limits

The following is an independent reading exercise, not a report of fieldwork.

A leaf plate can be drawn directly from observation, leaving the plant in place. The aim is to capture identifying structure and context rather than inventing a precise species from one image.

Consult a reliable regional field guide before assigning a name. Keep uncertainty on the page and avoid conclusions about edibility, toxicity or medicinal use from the sketch.

Do not collect protected plants or specimens where local rules prohibit it. Never taste an unidentified plant.
