Can I identify a wild plant from this website?

No. Illustrations are stylized and are not identification keys.

Can I collect any specimen?

No. Respect protected areas, land access rules, and species conservation requirements.

Is any personal location stored?

No. The pages are static and do not request geolocation.

What should I check in this material: Margin?

Smooth, serrated or lobed edge, observed rather than assumed. Example: From a permitted path, sketch the general outline and the way the veins divide. Compare one mature leaf with another on the same plant without damaging either.

What should I check in this material: Venation?

Visible branching of the main and side veins. Example: Record growth form, approximate leaf size, position on the stem, date and habitat. If photographing, include a wider view to retain the context of the plant.

What should I check in this material: Context?

Tree, shrub or herb and the surroundings in which it grows. Example: Consult a reliable regional field guide before assigning a name. Keep uncertainty on the page and avoid conclusions about edibility, toxicity or medicinal use from the sketch.

What are the limits of this exercise?

Do not collect protected plants or specimens where local rules prohibit it. Never taste an unidentified plant.
