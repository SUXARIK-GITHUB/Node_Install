Natural History

Stories

The shape of a leaf records weather, time, and place without writing a single word.

01

Leaf architecture

Veins distribute water and strengthen the blade. Their branching patterns provide useful visual clues, though identification should rely on multiple features.

02

Seed dispersal

Wind, water, and animals move seeds in very different ways. Studying the surrounding habitat is as important as examining the seed itself.

03

Pressed specimens

Drying a specimen carefully preserves a record of its shape. Collection must follow local conservation rules and should avoid protected plants.

04

Seasonal change

The color of a leaf reflects changing pigments and environmental conditions. A dated sketch can communicate details that a single label misses.
