Questions

Answers on a star chart, the lunar cycle and related reading from Celestial Index.

A star chart: How is a star chart oriented?

A star chart is useful only when the time and location match.

The lunar cycle: Why does the Moon change phase?

The lunar phase depends on the relative positions of the Sun, Earth and Moon. It is not caused by Earth's shadow during ordinary monthly changes.

Light pollution: What makes light pollution relevant to observing?

Compare two hypothetical observing places for glare and horizon obstruction while keeping safe access in view. Do not claim that a darker image proves better conditions.

What should I check in this material: Prediction?

Date, location and almanac are named. Example: Consult a current almanac for your locality and record local time, time zone, moon phase and the date of the source.

What should I check in this material: Observation?

Weather and drawing are separate. Example: Sketch the visible illuminated edge from a safe location, adding notes about clouds and light pollution without inventing surface features.

What should I check in this material: Confidence?

Missing visibility is not filled in. Example: Compare your sketch with the predicted phase, noting uncertainty in orientation and whether the Moon was actually visible.

What are the limits of this exercise?

Never aim binoculars or a telescope at the Sun without certified solar protection. This static page supplies no live ephemerides.
