Questions

Answers on bold hierarchy, a two-color poster and related reading from Poster Study.

Bold hierarchy: How does hierarchy change a poster?

Bold hierarchy lets a single message survive a crowded street.

A two-color poster: Can two colors carry a clear message?

Two inks can create rich relationships through overprint and contrast. Test the actual printing process rather than predicting every mixed color on screen.

Letter spacing: How does letter spacing affect reading?

Print a few lines with intentionally tight and loose tracking, then note where individual letters begin to merge. Keep the source text identical.

What should I check in this material: Headline?

Main subject is readable at a glance. Example: Write a fictitious event clearly labelled as a layout exercise and arrange its title, date and location as plain text.

What should I check in this material: Details?

Date and location remain findable. Example: Produce two layouts that keep the same wording but change size and alignment; check their reading order at thumbnail size.

What should I check in this material: Contrast?

Essential meaning survives without color. Example: Test both at high zoom and in monochrome so the information remains understandable without color cues.

What are the limits of this exercise?

Do not present a sample event as a real public announcement. Respect image and font licensing when producing a finished poster.
