Questions

Answers on backup rotation, cold storage and related reading from Quiet Storage.

Backup rotation: What belongs in a backup rotation?

Rotation protects against accidental deletion only when old copies remain recoverable.

Cold storage: What makes cold storage recoverable?

Cold storage may be slow to access, so retrieval time belongs in recovery planning. Confirm that the media and decryption method remain available.

Recovery points: How should recovery points be chosen?

Draw a recovery timeline that includes discovery, authorization, restoration and validation. Mark the point at which normal writes can safely resume.

What should I check in this material: Inventory?

Names, sizes and expected hashes are recorded. Example: Create a small directory of test documents and record file names, sizes, checksums and the intended destination medium.

What should I check in this material: Media?

Copy is independently accessible. Example: Copy the directory to a disconnected device and verify the checksum list from the copy rather than trusting the transfer dialog.

What should I check in this material: Restore?

Files are usable at a new location. Example: Restore into a separate empty folder and note the required software, access permissions and any missing filename metadata.

What are the limits of this exercise?

Do not include production secrets or treat one backup medium as sufficient resilience. Verify recovery periodically.
