Questions

Answers on color relationships, a useful grid and related reading from Fourth Form.

Color relationships: What makes two colors work together?

Pair a quiet neutral with one vivid color before adding more accents.

A useful grid: What does a useful grid clarify?

A grid explains alignment but should not turn every block into the same rectangle. Mark the repeating baseline and deliberately choose where to interrupt it.

Prototype studies: How should a small prototype be documented?

Cut a small paper prototype of one everyday object, photograph it from two viewpoints and record the practical issue each view reveals.

What should I check in this material: Wide view?

Recognizable environment and scale cues. Example: Place one object against a neutral background and make a wide frame that clearly shows its surroundings and scale.

What should I check in this material: Medium view?

Relationship between outline and background. Example: Create a medium and a close frame without changing the light. Record what information disappears when the object fills more of the picture.

What should I check in this material: Detail?

Texture without losing the identity of the object. Example: Arrange the frames in a row and ask which communicates context, material and detail; note that preference is not a measured performance result.

What are the limits of this exercise?

These are illustrative compositions, not commissioned design work. Check rights before publishing third-party artwork or photographs.
