Method and limits

The following is an independent reading exercise, not a report of fieldwork.

A compact exercise in visual hierarchy needs only two rectangles and one circle. The forms are intentionally abstract: their spacing, order and overlap are the material of the study.

In the third, overlap the smaller rectangle with the circle. Compare the edges that remain visible and write down which form a new viewer notices first.

These are proposed exercises, not measurements from a real exhibition or claims about a particular artist. Results depend on scale and presentation.
