What should I check in this material: Source?

Keep the same recording and playback format. Example: Choose a legally available recording you know well. Use the same source and speakers or headphones throughout, and start at a comfortable low volume.

What should I check in this material: Level?

Stay at a comfortable volume; protect hearing. Example: Listen once for the relationship between foreground instruments and room ambience. Write a few neutral observations about balance, transients and the space between sounds.

What should I check in this material: Notes?

Separate audible details from preference and expectation. Example: Repeat with a modest adjustment only if safe and comfortable. Avoid treating a subjective impression as a calibrated loudness measurement or changing multiple devices at once.

What are the limits of this exercise?

Protect your hearing from excessive volume. No audio tracks or commercial recordings are hosted by this page.
