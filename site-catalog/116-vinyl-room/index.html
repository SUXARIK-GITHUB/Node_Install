The first groove

A clean stylus, balanced tracking force and a level surface are the fundamentals of safe playback. Equipment care matters more than fashionable accessories.

02

Sleeve as an object

A record cover has scale and tactile presence that thumbnails cannot reproduce. Typography and artwork shape our memory of the sound.

03

A listening shelf

Grouping records by mood or discovery can be more rewarding than strict genre. A personal catalogue should make rediscovery easy.

04

Proper storage

Keep sleeves upright, away from heat and moisture, and avoid touching the playing surface. Small habits extend the life of a collection.
