Astronomy & Observation

Stories

There are useful stories in the sky, but they begin with careful records rather than guesses.

01

Moonlight and contrast

The Moon can wash out faint stars and change what is visible. Planning an evening observation begins with lunar phase and weather.

02

Finding direction

The apparent path of stars depends on latitude and time. A sky map is useful only when correctly oriented.

03

Writing an observation

Record the time, conditions, instrument and what was actually visible. An honest note is more valuable than a dramatic description.

04

Light around us

Shielded outdoor lights reduce glare and preserve the darkness needed for observing the sky.
