Does this calculate live star positions? — Celestial Index

Does this calculate live star positions?

No. Diagrams are educational artwork and not astronomical predictions.

Is it safe to observe the Sun?

Never look directly at the Sun through an optical instrument without certified solar protection.

Do the pages require internet access?

No. All resources are local static files.

What should I check in this material: Time?

Date, local clock and time zone. Example: Check a current local astronomical almanac and select a safe observing time. Write the date, time zone and approximate viewing direction before making a sketch.

What should I check in this material: Sky?

Visible illumination and conditions. Example: Draw the illuminated edge and visible shadow boundary with no attempt to manufacture surface detail. Note haze, clouds and artificial lighting separately.

What should I check in this material: Confidence?

Observations separate from chart-based predictions. Example: Repeat later in the month. Compare shape, time of day and the position relative to the horizon, remembering that a single sketch cannot determine all orbital properties.

What are the limits of this exercise?

Never observe the Sun through an unfiltered telescope or binoculars. A static page cannot provide current rise times or local visibility.
