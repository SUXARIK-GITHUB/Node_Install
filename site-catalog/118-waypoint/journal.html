Outdoor & Navigation

Stories

A good route begins long before the first step, with a map and a question about the terrain.

01

Before the trail

A planned route should account for daylight, elevation, terrain and an exit option. Share the plan with a trusted person before departure.

02

Contours and slopes

Close contour lines mean a steeper climb. Reading the terrain from a map helps anticipate effort before a hike.

03

Weather windows

A pleasant forecast at the trailhead does not imply identical conditions at higher elevations. Check regional advisories.

04

Leave the landscape intact

Follow established paths where possible and bring waste back. Observe wildlife without approaching or feeding it.
