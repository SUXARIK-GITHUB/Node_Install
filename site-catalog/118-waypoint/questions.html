Does this include live navigation?

No. Sketches are illustrative and cannot replace a map or a navigation device.

Are the trails real?

No specific route or accessible destination is promised by this sample journal.

Are current warnings included?

No. Consult official advisories and forecasts.

What should I check in this material: Navigation?

Paper or offline map with an identified return route. Example: Identify the start, destination and nearest safe exit. Confirm that the route is legally accessible, check elevation changes and note any stream crossings or exposed paths.

What should I check in this material: Conditions?

Weather, path closures and hazards checked on the day. Example: Check current weather, warnings and daylight. Choose a return deadline with spare time, carry navigation independent of cellular data and tell someone the intended route.

What should I check in this material: Decision?

A turnaround rule written down before departure. Example: Afterward, record actual conditions, route changes and any confusing markings. A qualitative account is useful when it clearly distinguishes a personal observation from route data.

What are the limits of this exercise?

This exercise is not a substitute for local guidance or professional planning for exposed, remote or technically demanding terrain.
