Questions

Answers on line length, hierarchy and scale and related reading from Type Workshop.

Line length: How long should a text line be?

Line length affects the effort required to find the next line.

Hierarchy and scale: What creates a clear heading hierarchy?

Hierarchy is easier to follow when heading sizes and spacing use a limited scale. Check the result on small screens and with longer translations.

Pairing type: How can two typefaces work together?

Pair two accessible typefaces in a sample article and label the role of each. Avoid pairing solely because their names belong to different categories.

What should I check in this material: Measure?

Line length recorded consistently. Example: Write a short original passage with punctuation, digits and a long word so the layout must handle varied characters.

What should I check in this material: Rhythm?

Spacing and punctuation remain legible. Example: Set it twice with the same font and size while adjusting only the text measure and line spacing.

What should I check in this material: Reflow?

No clipped text at high zoom. Example: Check ragged edges, broken words, keyboard navigation and reflow at 200% browser zoom.

What are the limits of this exercise?

Typography preferences depend on reader, language and device. A sample cannot certify accessibility by itself.
